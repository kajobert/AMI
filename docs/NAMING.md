# AMI Naming Standard

**Status:** DESIGN

AMI names communicate architectural role.

## Capability side: `AMI-*`

Names beginning with `AMI-` identify reusable capabilities, infrastructure or system body/tooling. Examples:

- `AMI-Knowledge-Core`
- `AMI-Kernel`
- `AMI-Protocol`

A capability does not have to be a standalone process. Archaeology may show that some concepts belong inside another module rather than becoming repositories of their own.

## Entity side: `*-AMI`

Names ending in `-AMI` identify persistent entities or identities participating through AMI. Example:

- `Sophia-AMI`

Within AMI vocabulary, the entity side may be described as the identity or consciousness layer: continuity, memory, agency, relationships and participation that are not reducible to one replaceable inference model.

Using the word `consciousness` is a project convention, not proof of any particular philosophical or biological theory of subjective experience.

## Historical names

Historical repositories are not renamed or promoted automatically. They remain evidence until archaeology determines what should be carried forward.

## Guiding principle

> **Trust instead of Authority.**

Names identify roles; they do not grant trust. Trust must come from evidence, provenance, tests and observed contribution.
